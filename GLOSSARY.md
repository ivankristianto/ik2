# ivankristianto.com

The personal engineering blog of Ivan Kristianto: long-form writing, notes, and the projects and talks behind them.

## Language

**Article**:
A single piece of published writing on the blog, read on its own page.
_Avoid_: Post (except when talking about the WordPress type), blog entry, story

**Format**:
The kind of writing an Article is: a guide, a note, or an experiment.
_Avoid_: Type, kind, category (when the format is meant)
