About Ivan Kristianto

I'm a web engineer who works on WordPress, performance, AI, and developer tooling. This site is a public notebook for tutorials, experiments, talks, and notes from my work on the web platform.

My interests sit at the intersection of publishing platforms, open source, browser technology, and the small tools that help developers build better user experiences.

I've worked on content publishing platforms for over a decade. Currently a Senior Web Engineer at Human Made, previously at 10up. I contribute to open source, speak at WordPress and web-tech events, and have helped grow the WordPress community in Indonesia since 2015.

Today, I'm focused on learning in public: building small tools, testing new browser APIs, documenting practical workflows, and sharing what I learn here.

Current focus

  • WordPress block themes and editor experience
  • Web performance for large publishers
  • AI-assisted development workflows
  • Browser APIs: View Transitions, Interactivity API, scroll-driven animations
  • Developer tooling and CLI ergonomics
