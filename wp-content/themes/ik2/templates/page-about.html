About Ivan Kristianto

I’m a web engineer who explores WordPress, AI, performance, and developer tooling. I use this site as a public notebook for technical tutorials, experiments, talks, and notes from my work on the web platform.

My interests sit at the intersection of publishing platforms, open-source software, browser technology, and the small tools that help developers build better user experiences.

I have worked on content publishing platforms for over a decade — currently as a Senior Web Engineer at Human Made, and previously at 10up. I contribute to open source, speak at WordPress and web-tech events, and have helped grow the WordPress community in Indonesia since 2015.

Today, I’m focused on learning in public: building small tools, testing new browser APIs, documenting practical workflows, and sharing what I learn here.

Current focus

  • WordPress block themes and editor experience
  • Web performance for large publishers
  • AI-assisted development workflows
  • Browser APIs — View Transitions, Interactivity API, scroll-driven animations
  • Developer tooling and CLI ergonomics
