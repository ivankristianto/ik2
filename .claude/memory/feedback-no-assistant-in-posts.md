Never mention Ivan's AI assistant in a blog post, not by name ("Rei") and not as "my AI assistant". On 2026-11-09 he asked to cut "I asked Rei, my AI assistant, to run the tests and check what the homepage loads" from post 2154: "lets never mention about my assistant".

**Why:** he doesn't want the post to be about who ran the checks; the reader needs the result.

**How to apply:** when research says the assistant ran a test, write the result as a fact with its date ("On November 9 the live homepage had zero external stylesheets"), not "Rei checked" and not "I ran" (that would claim work he didn't do). Rule is in blog-draft SKILL.md. See [[feedback-blog-voice]] and [[feedback-no-personal-ops-details]].
