Don't publish Ivan's own operational specifics in a post: the exact expiry date of a token, vault IDs, account names, Keychain item contents. Say "the token expires" or "runs out after 90 days" instead of the date.

**Why:** 2026-10-31, the 1Password service account post repeated the token's expiry date in five places. Ivan: "too granular and personal. no need to announce publicly."

**How to apply:** In blog-draft and blog-review, treat dates and identifiers that describe Ivan's live setup (not the story's timeline) as personal. The story date ("On October 31, 2026 I asked...") stays. See [[feedback-blog-voice]].
